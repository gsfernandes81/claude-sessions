## The remote view: lazy history, /btw, /usage and task notifications

Changes to `claude-remote`, the hub's relay and the holder, from the owner's use of 0.13.6
(2026-11-02).

- **History loads as you scroll.** Opening a conversation sends the phone about five screens,
  the newest first, and it keeps five screens loaded above the view, fetching more as you
  scroll back, so you only wait if a flick reaches the top before they arrive; `Loading
  earlier messages…` shows there meanwhile. The container no longer reads a resumed conversation's
  whole history either: the holder reads the transcript backwards, a few whole turns at a
  time, only when asked. On this 12 MB conversation each stretch took 10 to 80 ms to read and
  4 to 7 KB to send; the whole history, scrolled back to its first line, was 322 KB.
- **Flick scrolling no longer stutters.** Every wheel step laid out the whole conversation
  again, 50 ms on a long one; the layout is now cached and a step takes 0.04 ms.
- **`/btw` answers.** A side question takes 6 to 11 seconds, and the phone dropped a link that
  said nothing for 5 after a send, so the answer went to a dead connection. The relay now
  answers every message at once, which also keeps slow renames and offloads from dropping the
  link. If the link drops anyway, the panel says to ask again.
- **`/usage`** shows as typed, with Claude's report under it. Claude echoes a command it runs
  itself in a `<command-name>` wrapper after its reply; the phone showed the wrapper, below the
  reply, and the send spinner never stopped.
- **Background task notifications** show as their one-line summary, grey, not as XML.
- **Up takes queued prompts back** with text already typed, above it, as Claude Code does. It
  worked only on an empty prompt.
- **A tall prompt scrolls.** Past half the screen, the input box shows half the screen's rows
  and follows the cursor, `↑` or `↓` marking rows with more beyond them.

The wire gains `w` and `r` in `open`, `new` and `re`, the client's `older`, the relay's
`more` and `old`, and the holder's `older` header field and `_older` request. A holder from
before this release still works, its history kept in its log as before. A client from before
it is not shown a resumed conversation's history. For infra: pin this release. The hub must be
rebuilt, and the client installed again on the phone and WSL with it.
