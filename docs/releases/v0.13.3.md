## The remote view: Claude Code's keys and lists on the phone

`claude-remote` and the hub's relay, after the owner's second day on the phone
(2026-11-01 and 02). Each behaviour was checked against Claude Code 2.1.311 by driving it in a
pty and reading its screen, as recorded in `docs/remote-view.md`.

- **Esc as in Claude Code.** A prompt nothing has answered yet goes back into the input and
  off the transcript. A prompt with a reply already drawn, or with another prompt waiting
  behind it, stays, under `⎿  Interrupted · What should Claude do instead?`. Esc also works on
  a prompt that is still spinning.
- **Prompts waiting behind a turn:**
  - each shows `ctrl+x ctrl+s to send now` under it, and Ctrl+X Ctrl+S sends it now;
  - Up in an empty prompt, which reads `Press up to edit queued messages`, takes them back
    to edit;
  - claude takes one in between tool calls by itself, and the phone draws it there.
- **Pastes.** A paste over 800 characters or two line breaks stands in the prompt as
  `[Pasted text #N +L lines]`. Backspace removes it whole, and sending puts the paste back.
- **Slash commands.**
  - Typing one shows Claude Code's suggestion list over the prompt: Up and Down choose, Tab
    completes it with its argument hint, Enter sends it, Esc hides the list.
  - A known command is lavender wherever it is in the prompt.
- **The prompt.** It soft-wraps at words. Up and Down move between its rows, then through
  what was sent, and Down past the newest gives back what was being typed.
- **Claude's replies** no longer start a wrapped line with a space.
- **The home's `c`** offloads a loaded session and archives an offloaded one, through the
  container's agent as the door's does. The home no longer offers a shell or a pi session,
  which need the door's terminal.
- **A dropped link** shows `⠋ Reconnecting… (8s)`: after the status line where both fit, in
  its place where they do not, on the home's load line too.

The wire: these are all additive, and an older peer ignores what it doesn't know.
- New client ops: `offload`, `archive`, `now` and `unqueue`; `say` gains `u`, the prompt's
  uuid.
- New relay ops: `did` and `back`; `meta` gains `sc`, the slash commands; `models` gains
  `c`, the commands with their descriptions.
- The relay sends each prompt to claude with a uuid, and reads claude's `command_lifecycle`
  lines.

For infra: pin this release. The relay changed, so the hub must be rebuilt as well as the
client installed again on the phone and WSL. Nothing changes in the containers' install, the
registry, or `--version`.
