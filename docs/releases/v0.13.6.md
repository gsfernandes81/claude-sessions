## The remote view: slow opens and /rename

Fixes to `claude-remote` and the hub's relay, from the owner's use of 0.13.5 (2026-11-02). The
owner resumed a long conversation from the phone: nothing typed was sent, and `/rename` did
nothing.

- **A slow open no longer loses what is typed.** Resuming a long conversation can take more than
  5 seconds, which was the longest the phone waited for any answer. Past that it cut the link
  and dialled again, and every prompt went to a relay that had no conversation open. The phone
  now waits up to 60 seconds for an open, and the relay acknowledges an open as soon as it
  starts. Anything typed while a conversation opens is held with its spinner and sent once
  the conversation is open. If the link drops during an open, the phone resumes the
  conversation again, or, for a new one, goes home, where the session is listed if it started.
- **`/rename` works before claude's first reply.** Until claude has replied once, the phone does
  not know its session id. The rename was sent with an empty id, which claude refuses; the id
  is now left out, which claude accepts.
- **Claude's refusals are shown.** When claude refuses a request from the phone, the reason
  now appears on the phone instead of the request silently doing nothing.

The wire gains one message, `opening`, from the relay to the client; an older client ignores
it. For infra: pin this release. The hub must be rebuilt for the rename fix and the refusals;
the client installed again on the phone and WSL is enough for the open fix.
