## The remote view reconnects after a drop

- **A dropped link comes back.** A new link had 5 seconds to answer, and ssh's handshake
  through cloudflared from a phone often takes longer, so every redial was cut just before
  it answered and the view said Reconnecting for ever. A new link now gets 30 seconds to say
  hello. Once connected, the 5-second dead-link check holds as before.
- **Redials are spaced as meant.** An ssh the client had already cut was counted down again
  when it ended, doubling the backoff twice per failed dial (1, 4, 16, 30 seconds). It now
  doubles once.

Client only: the wire, the relay, the holder and the registry are unchanged. For infra: pin
this release as usual. Reinstall the phone client (`claude-remote`) to get the fix.
