## Swap beside RAM, peers named by what they are working on, and arrows on the phone's homes

- **Swap has its own figure, beside RAM**: `RAM: 1.3G / 2.0G, Swap: 1.0G / 4.0G, Load: 2.8,
  claude-sessions`, in each session's status line, the remote view's footer and the home's
  load line. Swap comes from the same place as RAM: the pool's or the container's cgroup
  (`memory.swap.current` against `memory.swap.max`, capped at the host's swap), else the
  host's `/proc/meminfo`. It turns yellow and red at RAM's 70% and 85%, and reads
  `Swap: none` on a machine with no swap. At 40 columns the remote view and the home squeeze
  the line to `RAM 1.3/2.0G, Swap 1.0/4.0G, Load 2.8`, so a phone keeps all three figures.
- **Peers are listed by repo/slot and their conversation's title**, in other containers and
  in the same one, so `ListAgents` says what each session is working on. A rename keeps the
  names it had shown before.
- **On the phone's two homes, Right goes in as Enter does and Left comes back out as Esc
  does.** Left never leaves the client from the home that lists every container.
- **A key redials a dropped link at once**, at the door and on the phone, instead of waiting
  out the retry delay; the phone's backoff then starts again from one second.

For infra:

- **Nothing to change.** The drop-in, the volumes and the variables are as in 0.18.4. A
  container's header now carries `swap` and the router's roster names each repo. Both are
  read tolerantly: from an agent or hub not yet on 0.18.5, swap reads as `?` until it
  restarts onto this release.
