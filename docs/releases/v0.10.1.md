## Sessions by last activity, and two fixes from the first pi slots

**The menu orders each group by last activity** (owner, 2026-11-01): most recent first, by
the age in the right-hand column and nothing else. A session waiting for you keeps its `!`
and amber, and an unread one its bold, but neither is moved to the top any more.
`claude-sessions list` uses the same order. Rows still keep their places while the menu is
open, and a new row, or one that changes group, still joins the top of its group.

**pi's events reach the hook one at a time**, in the order pi fired them. Before this, a
fresh pi slot could read as waiting on a prompt that had already been answered.

**The mail-read and mail-reply skills load in pi.** Their descriptions are quoted now,
because pi reads a skill's frontmatter as strict YAML and dropped both.

Nothing changes for infra beyond the pin: no new variables, and nothing new on the wire or in
the registry.
