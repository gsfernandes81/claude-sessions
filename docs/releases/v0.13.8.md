## The remote view: artifacts, /rc, and prompts on their way after a reconnect

Changes to the holder, the hub's relay and `claude-remote`, from the owner's use of 0.13.7
(2026-11-02).

- **Artifacts work in remote sessions.** Headless claude leaves the Artifact tools out unless
  told who runs it. The holder now starts claude as Claude Desktop starts one it drives over
  ssh (`CLAUDE_CODE_ENTRYPOINT=ssh-remote`), which brings `Artifact`, `ArtifactComments` and
  `ArtifactData` back. This applies to sessions started after the upgrade: a remote session
  already running keeps the tools it started with until it is restarted.
- **`/rc`** (or `/remote-control`) turns on Claude's Remote Control for the session, as Claude
  Code's does. Its claude.ai/code link is said in the conversation and copied, to open in the
  Claude app, where a screenshot can be attached. `/rc` again turns it off.
- **A prompt on its way survives a reconnect.** A prompt sent while Claude was busy is only
  echoed when the turn takes it in, so reconnecting showed the agent working with no sign of
  the prompt. The phone is now told of prompts still waiting, and shows them as queued with
  their spinner.

The wire gains the client's `rc`, and the relay's `rc` and `wait`. For infra: pin this
release. The hub must be rebuilt, and the client installed again on the phone and WSL.
