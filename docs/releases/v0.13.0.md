## The remote view: agents, side questions, copying, and leaving

New features in `claude-remote` and the relay, from the MVP's deferred list and the owner's
asks of 2026-11-01, plus fixes from the first day on the phone.

- **Subagents and workflows are drawn as Claude Code draws them**, on their own instead of
  summed into a run of tool calls. An agent is `● Explore(what it was asked)`, with its last
  two tool calls and a count of the rest under it while it runs. When it finishes, that
  becomes `Done (4 tool uses · 21.6k tokens · 7.6s)`. A workflow shows its phase, how many
  of that phase's agents are done, and what the running ones are doing, then its totals.
  The dot turns green when it ends well and grey when it fails or is stopped, and the line
  under it says which.
- **`/btw QUESTION` asks a side question** without touching the conversation, as Claude
  Code's does. A short-lived claude answers it: a fork of the conversation, never saved,
  with no tools, hooks or MCP servers, on the conversation's model. The answer shows in its
  own panel until Space, Enter or Esc. A container with no room for another claude refuses
  the question.
- **`/copy [N]`** copies the Nth response back (1 is the latest). A response is a turn's
  text without its tool calls. When the response has code blocks, a picker offers the
  whole response or one block.
- **Ctrl+\\ and `/disconnect`** go from a conversation to the home, even with a dialog
  open, and the session keeps running, as zmx's detach key does in `ssh dev`. Ctrl+\\ on the
  home quits the client.
- **Ctrl+O and `/expand`** switch the transcript between tool calls summed up and every
  call drawn on its own with the first lines of its result.
- **One wheel step scrolls three lines under Windows Terminal** and one everywhere else.
  Termux sends a step per line a finger moves; Windows Terminal sends one per notch.
- **Fixed: a phone-driven session could not be opened from the all-containers home.**
  The home dropped the flag that says the phone drives a session, so Enter refused it as
  if it ran in a terminal. Opening it from its container's own view worked.
- **Fixed: an occasional failure in the pty tests' idle-bytes check.** It caught the
  minute's allowed redraw when its window opened just after a minute boundary. Test-only.

The wire, all additions that an older peer skips:
- A subagent or workflow is added as kind `agent`, and `prog {id, p, st}` replaces the
  lines under it. An older client draws neither.
- The client sends `btw {x}`; the relay answers `btw {x}` or `btw {e}`. An older relay
  refuses it with `err`, which a new client shows in the panel.
- The container gains `claude-sessions remote btw SLOT [MODEL]`, with the question on its
  stdin.

For infra: pin this release. The hub's relay and every container's binary come from it as
usual. Each phone or WSL machine needs the new static binary installed as `claude-remote`.
