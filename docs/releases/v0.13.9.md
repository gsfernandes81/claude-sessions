## Refusals to open, the same at the door and in the remote view

From driving the door and `claude-remote` side by side through the same session states
(2026-11-03). Each now refuses what the other refuses, in the same words.

- **The door no longer attaches a session the remote view drives.** It attached to the
  holder's status line, which drops what is typed: a dead end. It now says why, as the phone
  does for a terminal's session: `"…" runs in the remote view: open it from claude-remote, or
  end it there to open it here`. `c` still offloads it, and an offloaded one still resumes at
  the terminal.
- **No room offers nothing to offload** (owner, 2026-11-03). Mockup 4 offered the idlest
  session with `y offload, then open`; it now points at `c`, at the door and on the phone,
  where it used to say to go to ssh first.
- **The remote view's refusals name the session by its title**, as the door's do, not by its
  slot (`claude-1`).
- **A resumed remote session's status line says idle** once claude starts, not `starting`
  until its first prompt.

The agent drops the `offload_then_open` op; a hub that still sends it is told the op is
unknown and nothing is stopped. A new hub reads an older agent's offer and ignores it. For
infra: pin this release. The hub must be rebuilt; containers' agents take the door's refusal
when restarted onto it. The phone client is unchanged and need not be reinstalled.
