# The home as the only page — four drawn options (2026-11-08)

**Not approved. Nothing here binds.** The approved screens are [`mockups.md`](../mockups.md); this
is the set the owner asked for on 2026-11-08 ("Let's build a unified single page tree on home.
Suggest a few designs … Mockups only at this stage"), drawn so one can be chosen. When one is,
it goes into `mockups.md` with the owner's word and this file goes.

Today (mockup 12) the home lists each container with its **loaded** sessions on a tree, and
`Enter` on a container opens a second page, mockup 13, where Offloaded and the folded Archived
are. Every option below puts the offloaded and archived sessions on the home itself, so there is
no second page: `Esc` quits from the home, and `Esc back` leaves the hint line. They differ in
**where a session's state is said** — the branch it hangs from, or a word on its row — and in
**what shows the rest**: `Enter` on one branch, or a key for every container at once.

What stays as it is in all four: the header, the Containers heading, a container's row and its
summary in words, `!` and amber for a session waiting, bold for unread and dim for attached, the
age column, `n` `p` `s` acting in the cursor's container, `c` one step down, the fold line
`… N more` counting the sessions below it, and the footer. Every line was generated to its width
and asserted against it, as the approved set was: 40 columns for all, 80 for each option's home.
The glyphs are the approved set plus `│` (already in the dialog frames) for a branch that passes
a deeper one.

## Design A — branches that fold, one at a time

Each container's tree grows two branches, in the words the Archived heading already uses:
`Offloaded · 1 · Enter shows` and `Archived · 7 · Enter shows`, shut when the menu starts.
`Enter` on one opens it in place, its sessions a level deeper on `│  └─` (or `   └─` under
the last branch); `Enter` again shuts it. Loaded sessions hang bare from the container, as they
do now, so the state is never colour-only: a session under no labelled branch is loaded, one
under `Offloaded` is offloaded. `Enter` on a container's own row **folds the container** to
its row — the "focus on one container" of 2026-10-26, done by shrinking the others — and
`Enter` again unfolds it; a folded container is its row with nothing under it, which its
summary (`2 offloaded`) explains. The hint line is unchanged.

Costs: a line or two per container for the fold rows, drawn only where there is something to
fold; and resuming an offloaded session takes two keys, `Enter` on its branch and `Enter` on the
row, where mockup 13 took `Enter` on the container and `Enter` on the row — the same two.

## Design B — one flat tree, a word for the state, a key to filter

Every session hangs from its container on one tree, **ordered by age alone** as the 2026-11-01
rule says, loaded and offloaded interleaved. The state is a dim word before the title, in
`pi: `'s place: `off: ` for offloaded, `arch: ` for archived; a loaded session carries none, as
on today's home. The archive is **hidden by default**, and the Containers heading says so —
`── Containers · 4 · archived hidden ──`; `a` shows it in every container at once and hides it
again. One word joins the hint line, `a archived`, which fits the existing rows at both widths.
A second key for offloaded (`o offloaded`) is possible but costs a third hint row at 40 and a
second at 80, so it is not drawn; offloaded sessions are few and are what you resume, so they
are shown.

This is the fewest lines and the fewest keys: an offloaded session is one `Enter` away. Costs:
the words are abbreviations (`pi: ` is four columns; these are five and six), and they are the
owner's to rename; and the archive opens everywhere or nowhere — at 40×24 it floods the home
(B2), where A and C open one container's.

## Design C — A's branches, Offloaded open, `a` for every archive (recommended)

A's tree with two changes. **Offloaded is always open**: a labelled branch `Offloaded · 1` with
its sessions under it on `│  └─`, no fold — they are few, and they are what the owner comes to
resume, so one `Enter` reaches them. **Archived folds per container**, `Archived · 7 · Enter
shows`, shut at start, and the one new key `a` opens or shuts **every** archive at once, while
`Enter` still opens one. `Enter` on a container folds it, as in A. The hint line gains
`a archived`, which fits the existing rows at both widths, and the keys screen (C4) says what
the tree is.

This keeps the owner's hierarchy — container, then loaded / offloaded / archived — on one page,
says every state in words on the branch, and has both ways in: one archive with `Enter`, all of
them with `a`. Costs: one line for the Offloaded label where a container has any, one for the
Archived fold where it has any; at 40×24 four containers' trees do not all fit once an archive
is open, and the list folds (C2).

## Design D — by state across the fleet, the container a prefix

The other way round: the fleet's sessions in the three groups the container screen has —
Loaded, Offloaded, the folded Archived — with each row carrying its container as a dim prefix,
`infra-dev/`, the address `ListAgents` already uses for the fleet. A Containers group on top
keeps one row per container for `n`, `p`, `s` and for one that is not answering. Nothing new to
learn: it is mockup 13 widened to the fleet, with the existing fold.

Drawn as the contrast. It inverts the owner's call of 2026-10-26 (the container is the top of
the hierarchy), the prefix costs a title seven to ten columns at 40, and a container's sessions
are spread across three groups rather than read together.

## Decisions the chosen one needs, whichever it is

- **`Enter` on a container.** With no second page it is free; A and C spend it on folding the
  container. The alternative is to keep mockup 13 as a zoom that `Enter` still opens, which is
  then not quite one page.
- **A container not answering.** Drawn here with its error on a dim branch under it, cut to the
  width with no ellipsis, so the home says why without a page for it; `Enter` on the row would
  show mockup 14's full text. Or mockup 14 stays as it is and the branch is dropped.
- **What the menu remembers.** Proposed: nothing — every archive shut and every container
  unfolded whenever the menu starts, as the Archived heading is today. A fold opened stays open
  until shut or until it empties.
- **The cursor** rests on a session, a container, or a fold row, as it rests on the Archived
  heading today; it steps over labels that fold nothing (C's `Offloaded · 1`), headings and
  blank lines.
- **`c` on a fold row or a container row** says what `c` is for, as it does on a container row
  today.
- **The fold line** `… N more` counts sessions, not branches or container rows, as today.
- **Ordering** within a container stays the 2026-11-01 rule, most recent first by age, inside
  each branch; B applies it across states.
