## The home as a directory tree, with icons

H with an icon in front of every name, one cell wide, so the tree reads at a glance what the
words still say. **Folders get folders**: a container, `offloaded` and `archived` are drawn
with `▾` open or `▸` shut — the shut mark does what `…` did in H, and `…` goes back to the fold
line. **A session gets its state**: `!` waiting for you, in amber as today; `✻` thinking —
Claude Code's own spinner glyph, drawn still; `●` running a command or a monitor — Claude Code's
grey running bullet; `·` idle at its prompt — the dim middle dot the header already uses, so
the common state is the quietest mark. A session in `offloaded` or `archived` has no icon; its
folder says what it is. `pi: ` follows the icon, as it followed `!`. A shut container hiding a
waiting session is `▸ ! infra-dev`.

The column costs a title two columns everywhere, where `! ` took them only from a waiting
title; at 40 a title has 30 columns on the first level. Everything else is H: `Right` opens,
`Left` shuts or steps out, `Enter` opens a session or toggles a folder, `Esc` quits, nothing is
counted, and the list folds with `… N more` counting rows.

**The glyphs, measured** (2026-11-08, by the method of the `↵` check: the six faces' current
subsets downloaded from Google Fonts and their cmaps and advances read). The table is a
tiebreaker, not a veto: the owner's word is that what Claude Code draws works on these
terminals, and the same subsets lack `✻`, `⎿`, `❯` and `⏵`, which the approved remote view
draws. These are to be **tried on the owner's terminals** (owner, 2026-11-08), Termux and
Windows Terminal, and the ASCII column is what to fall back to, one glyph at a time.

| icon | means | Claude Code draws it | in the six faces | ASCII fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `▾` `▸` | folder open, shut | no; its `▲` is the same block | 3 of 6: JetBrains, Noto, Source | `-` `+` |
| `✻` | thinking | yes, its spinner | 0 of 6, like `⎿` and `❯` | `*` |
| `●` | running a command or a monitor | yes, the grey running bullet | 4 of 6: Fira, JetBrains, Noto, Source | `o` |
| `·` | idle, at its prompt | yes | 6 of 6 | itself |
| `!` | waiting for you | — | ASCII | itself |

Not chosen, and why: `○` for idle pairs with `●` but is missing from JetBrains Mono; `◯` is
double width in Noto; `▶` `▼` are emoji-presentation on some terminals and `▶` is double
width in Noto; the circle halves `◐` are in two faces; every hourglass, gear and flag is in
none.

**What the hook has to learn** for `✻` against `●`: the registry knows busy (`UserPromptSubmit`
to `Stop`) and waiting (`Notification`), not whether a tool is running. Claude Code's
`PreToolUse` and `PostToolUse` hooks, both async like the others, would set and clear a
`tool` field — its name, so a `Monitor` or a long `Bash` reads as running a command or a
monitor and everything else in a turn reads as thinking; pi's extension has the same two
events. Until then a busy session is `✻` throughout its turn.

## Decisions the chosen one needs, whichever it is

- **The icons** are the owner's to try; the table above gives each one's fallback. One that
  fails on a terminal is swapped for its ASCII, not dropped: the column stays, so the
  alignment does.
- **The spinner** for an action in progress (`⠋`, mockup 11) stays in the age column; the
  icon column never animates, so an idle menu still writes nothing but its load line.
- **What the menu remembers.** Proposed: nothing — every container open one level and every
  subdirectory shut whenever the menu starts, as the Archived heading is today. A directory
  opened stays open until shut, or until it empties, which shuts it.
- **The header.** Drawn without `4 containers`, as a count; the figures stay. The owner's to
  keep if the number is wanted.
- **The `!` on a container.** Drawn only while the waiting session is hidden in a shut
  container. The alternative is to keep it always, as mockup 12 has it, at the cost of saying
  it twice.
- **The cursor** rests on every row: a container, a subdirectory, a session. `c` on a
  directory says what `c` is for, as it does on a container row today.
- **The fold line** `… N more` counts rows, since a shut directory is a row; today it counts
  sessions.
- **Ordering** within a directory stays the 2026-11-01 rule, most recent first by age; the two
  subdirectories come after the loaded sessions, `offloaded` first.
- **The arrows on the phone.** The remote view's homes already take `Right` as `Enter` and
  `Left` as `Esc` (2026-11-08); here `Left` never quits, and `Right` on a session opens it as
  `Enter` does.
- **The footer** is four of 24 lines at 40 columns. One hint row, with `s shell` and `p new pi`
  left to the keys screen, would give the list a line back; not drawn, since those screens are
  the owner's.

## The home as a directory tree

**A container is a directory.** In it, as the files, its loaded sessions; then two
subdirectories, `offloaded` and `archived`, drawn only when there is something in them, dim and
lowercase because they are structure. The home opens with every container open one level (H1):
the loaded sessions showing, the two subdirectories shut. **A shut directory with something in
it ends in `…`** in the age column — the one glyph measured for the fold line, meaning here what
it means there: more, not drawn. An open directory has its contents under it; a container with
nothing in it is its name alone; one not answering says so, as today, and `Enter` on it is
mockup 14.

**The arrows walk the tree, and nothing is a `cd`.** `Right` on a shut directory opens it, on an
open one steps to its first entry. `Left` on an open directory shuts it, on anything else steps
out to the directory it is in. `Enter` on a session opens the session — resuming it from
`offloaded` or `archived` — and on a directory opens or shuts it, as a click does, so a terminal
without arrows loses nothing. No mode, no `Esc back`: `Esc` quits. The hint line is unchanged,
and the keys screen (H4) has the arrows.

**What is said in words**: `not answering`, `!` before a waiting title — or before a shut
container's name, for as long as the waiting session is hidden in it (H3) — `pi: `, the
directory a session is in, and `…` for shut-with-contents. Nothing is counted. Opening every
archive at once does not fit at 40×24, and the list folds as it does everywhere (H2), the fold
now counting rows, since a shut directory is a row too.

## The home as one page, nothing counted

**A container's row is its name.** It says something on the right only when there is a
problem — `not answering` — and carries `!` only when the session waiting for the owner is not
drawn under it. Its loaded sessions hang from it as they do today, so the state of what is on
screen is said by where it hangs and nothing is counted: no `4 containers` in the header, no
`3 loaded · 1 offloaded` on a row, no `Archived · 7`. Against the approved home (mockup 12) the
default screen (G1) is two lines shorter and every row is shorter.

**`Enter` on a container focuses it**, in place: its offloaded sessions appear under a dim
lowercase `offloaded` branch, its archive under a dim `archived` branch, shut, and every other
container folds to its name (G2). A folded container with a session waiting takes the `!`,
since the session is now hidden (G3). `Enter` on `archived` opens it (G4); on a session, opens
the session. `Esc back` joins the hint line while something is focused, and `Esc` unfocuses, as
it left mockup 13; `Enter` on the focused container's name does the same. One container is
open at a time, so the screen is bounded: three loaded, one offloaded and seven archived fit at
40×24 with the archive open.

**What is said in words**, so nothing is colour-only: `not answering` on the row, `!` before a
waiting title or a folded container, `pi: ` before a pi session, `offloaded` and `archived` on
the branch the sessions hang from, and `Esc back` on the hint line for as long as the home is

A container with nothing in it at all is its name, with nothing under it, focused or not.

**Unchanged**: the header's figures, the age column, bold for unread and dim for attached,

## What was noisy, and the rules the second round follows

Against the approved home (mockup 12), A1 draws the same four containers in seven more lines.
Five are fold rows that each say `Enter shows`; one is the ssh error; and the Containers
heading and the `3 loaded` in each summary were there already, saying what the header and the
tree say. B and C add labels or words to every row, D adds a prefix to every title. The noise
is repetition, not the tree. So, for the second round:

- **Say a thing once.** The header counts the containers, so no Containers heading. The tree
  shows the loaded sessions, so the summary does not count them. The keys screen says what
  `Enter` does, so no row does.
- **The row that folds is the fold.** A container's row is where its hidden sessions are
  counted and where `Enter` shows them; no row exists only to be opened.
- **Labels appear only in the exception.** `offloaded` and `archived · 7` are drawn inside a
  focused container and nowhere else, dim and lowercase, as structure.
- **One thing open at a time.** Focusing a container folds the others, so the screen is
  bounded: the home is never four trees deep at once.
- **Mockup 14 stays a page.** A container not answering is one row, and `Enter` on it says why.

One source of noise is outside the list: the footer is four of 24 lines at 40 columns, two of
them hints. A single hint row — `Enter open   n new   c close   ? keys`, the rest on the
keys screen — would give the list a line back on every screen. Not drawn; it changes mockups
the owner set, and is their call.

since the header already says `4 containers` and every row is one; the `3 loaded` in a
container's summary, since the three sessions hang right under it; every fold row, since the
container's own row is the fold; and the ssh error line, which stays on mockup 14. Changed:
**the right side of a container's row says only what is not drawn under it** — `1 offloaded
· 7 archived`, `nothing open`, `not answering` — so a row reads as "here is what is hidden",
and the words do the work the fold rows did.

offloaded sessions appear under a dim lowercase `offloaded` branch and its archive as a dim
`archived · 7` branch, shut — `Enter` on that opens it — and **every other container folds to
its row**, its summary now counting its loaded sessions too, since those are hidden. The hint
line gains `Esc back` for as long as something is focused, and `Esc` unfocuses, exactly as it
left mockup 13. So the focused state is mockup 13 drawn inside the home, bounded to one
container at a time: at 40×24 a container with three loaded, one offloaded and seven archived
fits with the archive open (E3). Labels are dim and lowercase because they are structure, not
headings; nothing says `Enter shows`, the keys screen (E4) does.

## Design A — branches that fold, one at a time

Each container's tree grows two branches, in the words the Archived heading already uses:
`Offloaded · 1 · Enter shows` and `Archived · 7 · Enter shows`, shut when the menu starts.
`Enter` on one opens it in place, its sessions a level deeper on `│  └─` (or `   └─` under
the last branch); `Enter` again shuts it. Loaded sessions hang bare from the container, as they
do now, so the state is never colour-only: a session under no labelled branch is loaded, one
under `Offloaded` is offloaded. `Enter` on a container's own row **folds the container** to
its row — the "focus on one container" of 2026-10-26, done by shrinking the others — and
`Enter` again unfolds it; a folded container is its row with nothing under it, which its
summary (`2 offloaded`) explains. The hint line is unchanged.

Costs: a line or two per container for the fold rows, drawn only where there is something to
fold; and resuming an offloaded session takes two keys, `Enter` on its branch and `Enter` on the
row, where mockup 13 took `Enter` on the container and `Enter` on the row — the same two.

## Design B — one flat tree, a word for the state, a key to filter

Every session hangs from its container on one tree, **ordered by age alone** as the 2026-11-01
rule says, loaded and offloaded interleaved. The state is a dim word before the title, in
`pi: `'s place: `off: ` for offloaded, `arch: ` for archived; a loaded session carries none, as
on today's home. The archive is **hidden by default**, and the Containers heading says so —
`── Containers · 4 · archived hidden ──`; `a` shows it in every container at once and hides it
again. One word joins the hint line, `a archived`, which fits the existing rows at both widths.
A second key for offloaded (`o offloaded`) is possible but costs a third hint row at 40 and a
second at 80, so it is not drawn; offloaded sessions are few and are what you resume, so they
are shown.

This is the fewest lines and the fewest keys: an offloaded session is one `Enter` away. Costs:
the words are abbreviations (`pi: ` is four columns; these are five and six), and they are the
owner's to rename; and the archive opens everywhere or nowhere — at 40×24 it floods the home
(B2), where A and C open one container's.

## Design C — A's branches, Offloaded open, `a` for every archive

A's tree with two changes. **Offloaded is always open**: a labelled branch `Offloaded · 1` with
its sessions under it on `│  └─`, no fold — they are few, and they are what the owner comes to
resume, so one `Enter` reaches them. **Archived folds per container**, `Archived · 7 · Enter
shows`, shut at start, and the one new key `a` opens or shuts **every** archive at once, while
`Enter` still opens one. `Enter` on a container folds it, as in A. The hint line gains
`a archived`, which fits the existing rows at both widths, and the keys screen (C4) says what
the tree is.

This keeps the owner's hierarchy — container, then loaded / offloaded / archived — on one page,
says every state in words on the branch, and has both ways in: one archive with `Enter`, all of
them with `a`. Costs: one line for the Offloaded label where a container has any, one for the
Archived fold where it has any; at 40×24 four containers' trees do not all fit once an archive
is open, and the list folds (C2).

## Design D — by state across the fleet, the container a prefix

The other way round: the fleet's sessions in the three groups the container screen has —
Loaded, Offloaded, the folded Archived — with each row carrying its container as a dim prefix,
`infra-dev/`, the address `ListAgents` already uses for the fleet. A Containers group on top
keeps one row per container for `n`, `p`, `s` and for one that is not answering. Nothing new to

Drawn as the contrast. It inverts the owner's call of 2026-10-26 (the container is the top of
the hierarchy), the prefix costs a title seven to ten columns at 40, and a container's sessions
are spread across three groups rather than read together.
